## B. non_human_self_talk_and_valence_tagging_for_all_life.md

### Dolphin "self-talk"
- Filatova, O. A., et al. (2024). "Dolphin self-talk: unusual acoustic behaviour of a solitary bottlenose dolphin." *Bioacoustics*. — solitary dolphin "Delle"; ~10,000 vocalizations; three signature whistles while isolated.

### Contrafreeloading & play
- Nature Portfolio (2024). "Overview of food contrafreeloading studies." *Nature*. — cross-species CFL table (bears, kea, crows, chimpanzees, giraffes, Siamese fighting fish, pigs; not cats/rats in reviewed studies).
- (2024). "Animal play and evolution: Seven timely research issues about enigmatic phenomena." *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews*. `[verify — author(s) omitted]`

### Dopamine systems across taxa
- Winding, M., et al. (2023) & Dorkenwald, S., et al. (2024). Striking parallels between fly and mammalian dopaminergic systems (Drosophila mushroom body ↔ vertebrate striatum; >90% of fly dopamine neurons target mushroom body). Grouped as one entry in the source. `[verify]`
- Benito-Gutierrez, E., et al. (2021) & Tolstenkov, O., et al. (2023). "The dopamine system from an evolutionary perspective." — dopamine in protovertebrates; mushroom body/basal ganglia parallels. Grouped as one entry in the source. `[verify]`

### Plant sociality & kin recognition
- (2024). "Leaves of three aromatic damaged plants show more sensitive kin recognition than their roots through volatile organic compounds." *ScienceDirect*. `[verify — journal/author omitted]`
- Hussain, A., Rodriguez-Ramos, J. C., & Erbilgin, N. (2019). "Spatial characteristics of volatile communication in lodgepole pine trees: Evidence of kin recognition and intra-species support." (Elsevier.) `[verify — journal not named]`

### Valence tagging & bacterial behavior/memory
- Park, H., Pontius, W., Guet, C. C., Marko, J., Emonet, T., & Cluzel, P. (2010). "Interdependence of behavioural variability and response to small stimuli in bacteria." *Nature*, 468, 819-823. — chemotaxis decision-making; also cited for clonal E. coli behavioral variation ("personalities").
- (2025). "Epigenetic cellular memory in Pseudomonas aeruginosa generates phenotypic variation in response to host environments." *PNAS*. — heritable `glpD` ON/OFF expression states. `[verify]`

### Transgenerational learned avoidance (C. elegans)
